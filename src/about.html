Our story

Nibature International Ministry began with a simple conviction: communities become stronger when faith is paired with practical care.

We bring people together across generations, offering encouragement, learning and support where it is needed most.

OUR VISION BEARER

Missionary Aimable Sematungo

Vision Bearer, Nibature International Ministry

Missionary Aimable Sematungo is the Vision Bearer of Nibature International Ministry. Originally from the Banyamulenge community in the Democratic Republic of Congo and currently based in Sweden, Aimable is a missionary driven by a powerful burden for global evangelism and spiritual restoration.

Through Nibature International Ministry, he works tirelessly to bridge communities, spread the Gospel of Jesus Christ, and bring practical hope to nations in need.
